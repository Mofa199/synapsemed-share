## 💡 Pro Tips

### Tip 1: Always Available
The floating button appears on **EVERY page** when you're logged in. You never have to search for it!

### Tip 2: Keyboard Shortcuts (Coming Soon)
- Press `Ctrl/Cmd + K` to open AI assistant
- Press `Escape` to close

For fast questions, use the floating button instead of navigating to the AI page.

### Tip 4: Detailed Work
For longer sessions (multiple flashcards, many questions), use the dedicated `/ai` page.

### Tip 5: Context Matters
The AI gives better answers when used in the right context (e.g., ask about topics while viewing that topic page).
