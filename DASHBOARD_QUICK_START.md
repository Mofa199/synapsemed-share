# 🎯 Student Dashboard - Quick Start Guide

## 🚀 **Access the Dashboard**
**URL**: http://localhost:3000/student/dashboard

---

## 📋 **New Features Overview**

### 1. 📚 **Bookmatcher AI**
**Where**: Top right of the header  
**Click**: "Bookmatcher" button

**How to Use**:
1. Enter a topic (e.g., "Cardiovascular Physiology")
2. Select difficulty level (Beginner/Intermediate/Advanced)
3. Choose subject area
4. Click "Find Books"
5. Get AI-matched book recommendations with relevance scores!

---

### 2. 📝 **My Notes**
**Where**: Main dashboard panel (right side, spans 2 columns)

**Create a Note**:
1. Click "+ New Note"
2. Enter title and choose category
3. Write content
4. Add tags (comma-separated)
5. Pin if important
6. Click "Save"

**Manage Notes**:
- 📌 **Pin/Unpin**: Click pin icon
- ✏️ **Edit**: Click edit icon
- 🗑️ **Delete**: Click trash icon
- 🔍 **Search**: Use search bar
- 🏷️ **Filter**: Click category buttons

**Categories**:
- General, Anatomy, Pharmacology, Study Plans, Clinical Notes, Research

---

### 3. 🧠 **Spaced Repetition**
**Where**: Dashboard overview section (middle card)

**Review Cards**:
1. See your stats:
   - **Red** = Overdue cards (need immediate review)
   - **Yellow** = Due today
   - **Green** = Upcoming
2. Click "Review Now"
3. Read the question, click to flip
4. Rate your recall:
   - ❌ **Forgot** - Resets the card
   - 😕 **Hard** - Review sooner
   - 🙂 **Good** - Normal interval
   - ✅ **Easy** - Longer interval

**AI Features**:
- Uses SuperMemo SM-2 algorithm
- Automatically schedules next review
- Adapts to your learning speed

---

### 4. 📅 **Question of the Day**
**Where**: Bottom left of dashboard

**Daily Practice**:
1. Read the clinical vignette
2. Select your answer (A, B, C, or D)
3. Click "Submit Answer"
4. Get instant feedback:
   - ✅ **Green** = Correct
   - ❌ **Red** = Your wrong answer
5. Read the detailed explanation
6. Build your streak! 🔥

**Notifications**:
- Click the bell icon to toggle daily reminders
- Get notified when new questions are available

**Gamification**:
- Earn **+10 XP** for correct answers
- Track your streak
- Difficulty badges

---

### 5. 📱 **Mobile Navigation**
**Where**: Bottom of screen (mobile only)

**Quick Access Icons**:
- 🏠 **Home** - Dashboard
- 📖 **Planner** - Study planning
- ▶️ **Videos** - Video library
- ❓ **Questions** - Question bank
- 🤖 **AI Tutor** - AI assistant

**Tips for Mobile**:
- Swipe to scroll through cards
- Tap cards for details
- Use bottom nav for quick jumps

## 🔥 **Pro Tips**

### For Better Note-Taking:
1. **Use tags** for easy searching
2. **Pin important notes** to keep them at the top
3. **Choose specific categories** for better organization
4. **Search across all fields** - works on title, content, and tags

### For Spaced Repetition:
1. **Be honest with ratings** - the algorithm works best with accurate feedback
2. **Review daily** - consistency is key
3. **Don't skip overdue cards** - they're crucial for retention
4. **Use "Forgot" liberally** - it's better to see a card more often

### For Question of the Day:
1. **Answer before checking** - don't just read the answers first
2. **Read explanations carefully** - even if you got it right
3. **Build a streak** - answer daily for motivation
4. **Enable notifications** - never miss a day

### For Bookmatcher:
1. **Be specific** - "ECG interpretation" is better than "cardiology"
2. **Use filters** - narrow by difficulty and subject
3. **Check relevance scores** - 90%+ are perfect matches
4. **Read the "why"** - understand why each book was recommended

## 🐛 **Troubleshooting**

### Notes not saving?
- Check internet connection
- Refresh the page
- Clear browser cache

### Cards not loading?
- Ensure you're logged in
- Check the stats panel for counts
- Refresh the page

### Bookmatcher slow?
- AI matching takes 2-3 seconds
- Wait for loading animation
- Try a more specific query

### Mobile nav not showing?
- Resize browser window to < 768px width
- Use mobile device or device emulator
- Check at bottom of screen
